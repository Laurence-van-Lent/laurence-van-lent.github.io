# Revision specification

## Organizing idea

The paper is primarily about voluntary managerial disclosure when later
confirmation is selective, delayed, and incomplete.

The observed relation between current discussion and later public evidence
depends on both:

1. what managers know and choose to discuss; and
2. the process through which underlying conditions become publicly visible.

This is the paper's selective-confirmation argument.

## Role of contestability

Contestability is a supporting and conditional mechanism.

A contestable statement gives outsiders a concrete object that could be
investigated or contradicted. Contestability does not necessarily produce a
stronger average signed relation in every environment.

The post-2020 evidence is consistent with contestability becoming more
consequential in a changed information environment. It does not establish a
causal monitoring effect and does not distinguish disclosure discipline from
greater outcome discovery.

## Evidence hierarchy

The manuscript should give greatest weight to the following results:

1. Executive HRDD discussion predicts later public evidence.
2. The relation is concentrated in executive rather than analyst speech.
3. The relation extends to labor adjudication and does not have a comparable
   positive relation with workplace-safety prosecution outcomes.
4. Executive discussion predicts consequential RepRisk-classified novel
   evidence, not only recurring coverage.
5. The discussion-to-evidence relation is stronger after 2020.
6. The post-2020 increase is concentrated in contestable executive language.
7. Independent human coders recognize the contestability measure as capturing
   language that gives outsiders a concrete object to investigate or
   contradict.

## Claims not established

The paper does not establish that:

- discussion causes later harm;
- the relevant information was unknown to every outsider;
- a novel RepRisk article marks the first occurrence of the underlying harm;
- an earlier sentence concerns the same specific problem as a later article;
- monitoring causally improves managerial disclosure;
- the post-2020 period is a clean monitoring treatment;
- the design separates disclosure discipline from outcome discovery;
- contestable language is universally more informative.

## Conceptual structure

The conceptual framework should distinguish three stages:

1. managers observe underlying risk and mitigation or remediation and choose
   what to discuss;
2. underlying conditions evolve after the call;
3. only some conditions enter the public record through investigation,
   reporting, litigation, adjudication, or enforcement.

Contestability enters because concrete language may give outsiders a more
usable basis for investigation or contradiction.

## Empirical structure

The main-text evidence should be organized approximately as follows:

1. baseline predictive relation;
2. executive versus analyst speech;
3. labor adjudication and the safety comparison;
4. novel versus recurring consequential public evidence;
5. the post-2020 change;
6. the contestable versus non-contestable executive decomposition;
7. human validation of the contestability classification.

FTSE visibility, Boohoo/Leicester, topic grids, and broader specification
families should be treated as supplementary evidence rather than as
independent identification of a common monitoring mechanism.

## Writing order

Revise the manuscript in this order:

1. conceptual framework;
2. introduction;
3. data and measurement;
4. empirical organization and interpretation;
5. conclusion;
6. abstract and title.

The abstract and title should be revised only after the rest of the paper is
stable.
